# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-26)

First public release: Homedial 1.0.0, extras 1.0.0 and Homedial Backdrop 1.0.0, free under the
Apache License 2.0.

- **Homedial:** room blending, a protected-room floor, freeze and overheat guards, gentle
  write pacing that survives restarts, humidity help in Cool, and a Reason sensor in plain words.
- **Extras:** Auto heat/cool around one target, fan timers, and an on-screen safety alert for Homedial Backdrop.
- **Homedial Backdrop:** 72 themes, including Directive Labs Navy (the default), Light, Dark and Calm, plus Seasons,
  Holidays, Travel postcards, a Line of the day and one card for the whole thermostat.
- Install through HACS or Marketplace (custom repository) or by hand. Downloads for the extras and Backdrop are on the
  Releases page.
- Formerly DLabs Thermostat and DLabs Core (private beta).
